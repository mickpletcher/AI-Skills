# Code of Conduct

## A welcoming community

AI Skills welcomes people of every experience level and background. Treat others with respect regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Participation should be safe and constructive for contributors and users alike.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and willing to explain technical decisions.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people. Accept corrections and constructive feedback.
- Respect different communication styles and accessibility needs. Offer a text alternative when useful.
- Credit others' work and preserve licenses and attribution.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the limits people set. Use sanitized examples instead of personal data or credentials.
- Keep contributions relevant to the project and distinguish tested results from assumptions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and when someone explicitly represents the project elsewhere. It applies to maintainers as well as contributors. The repository owner, [@mickpletcher](https://github.com/mickpletcher), is responsible for project moderation.

## Reporting a concern

For abusive content on GitHub, use the content's **Report content** menu. Choose **Report to repository admins** if that option is available. For violations of GitHub's rules, choose **Report abuse to GitHub Support** or follow [GitHub's abuse-reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). GitHub Support handles platform-rule violations, not every project-specific disagreement.

For another project conduct concern, you can open an issue titled **Request for private conduct contact**, asking @mickpletcher for a confidential reporting route. Include only the request for contact, not incident details or anyone's personal information. Wait for a private route before sharing details. This repository does not publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting address. If publicly requesting contact would put you at risk, do not post it; use GitHub's reporting options for platform abuse instead. Concerns involving the repository owner can also be reported to GitHub Support when they violate GitHub's rules.

Once a private route is established, share only the information needed to understand the concern, such as relevant links, what happened, and any immediate safety concerns. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues or pull requests. Suspected software vulnerabilities belong in the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask participants to change their behavior, remove or edit inappropriate content, lock a discussion, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should reflect the severity, context, and any repeated behavior. Serious threats or harassment may warrant immediate restriction without a prior warning.

Reports should be handled discreetly and information shared only as needed to assess and address the concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. A participant seeking reconsideration may ask for a private contact route using the process above and provide relevant context without reopening a public argument. This policy does not promise a response time or an independent appeals panel.
