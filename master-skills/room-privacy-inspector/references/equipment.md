# Optional equipment: iPhone or Android

Equipment is optional. Explain what a method measures before suggesting a product. No purchase, app install, active network scan, connection to unknown equipment or fixture tampering is authorized by this guide. Reading measurements the user already supplies is within scope. Keep use non-invasive, follow manufacturer safety instructions, avoid lasers/high-intensity light aimed at eyes, and leave professional-only methods to qualified specialists.

## Choose the signal, not a marketing promise

| Category | What it can contribute | Main limits and false positives | Phone dependence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Optical lens finder | Illuminated viewing can make some exposed lenses reflect light, potentially including unpowered cameras | Needs suitable line of sight/angle; concealed, coated, obstructed or tiny lenses can be missed. Glass, polished screws and other reflective surfaces can glint. Does not detect a microphone or establish recording | Often standalone with its own illumination/viewfinder; a charging USB socket does not mean a phone connection |
| RF detector | Indicates radio energy within the unit's supported bands and sensitivity | Phones, access points, neighboring rooms and ordinary radios trigger it. An indication does not identify a camera or its room. Offline, wired, silent, intermittent, shielded or out-of-band devices can be missed | Often standalone; app-enabled products require exact hardware/app verification. No RF measurement capability is added merely by installing a phone app |
| External thermal imager | Shows apparent surface temperature patterns that may help document a visible warm object | Chargers, wiring, pipes, reflections and normal electronics can create patterns. Cannot see through walls or reliably identify a recorder. Cool, insulated or inactive equipment may be missed. Does not detect microphones or all offline cameras | Standalone display units or phone accessories; verify exact platform variant, port, app and supported model |
| Supported external near-IR imaging | May show an emitting IR source within its specified spectral response | Not all cameras emit IR; ordinary remotes/sensors/illuminators can emit. No emission does not rule out a camera. Different from thermal imaging; neither establishes audio recording | Only consider a specific documented sensor/host combination; no generic built-in iPhone/Android compatibility claim |

LiDAR measures depth and is not an all-purpose recording-device finder. No category provides a clearance certificate or a measured detection percentage here. A microphone has no camera lens; do not transfer optical-lens claims to audio bugs. RF findings can concern transmitting devices but never establish that a microphone is present.

## Before any purchase recommendation

Ask for iPhone or Android, exact model, OS version, physical port, region/app-store availability, budget and intended use. For standalone equipment, phone compatibility may be **not applicable**; check its own charging, battery and safety requirements. Do not request unnecessary account or location details.

For phone-connected equipment, verify the **exact SKU/platform variant**, supported phone model and OS, connector (Lightning/USB-C/etc.), USB host/OTG requirements where relevant, app name/publisher/current availability and OS requirements, required permissions, power/case clearance, and manufacturer-supported adapters. The same USB-C plug does not establish iOS/Android interoperability. Wireless accessories also need verified app/protocol support. If any required fact is unknown, say compatibility is unverified and withhold a “works with your phone” or buy recommendation. A verified manufacturer claim is not a hands-on test.

Use current manufacturer documentation plus official app-store listings, reconcile conflicts and identify dated guidance. State what is measured, likely false positives, missing coverage, compatibility evidence and practical tradeoffs. Never borrow compatibility from an adjacent model or repeat seller claims such as “finds every spy device.” No named purchase recommendation is made by this reference; the examples below establish categories and compatibility pitfalls only.
