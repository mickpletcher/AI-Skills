# iPhone capability boundaries

Primary sources checked 2026-11-04. These sources describe platform features, not the effectiveness of hidden-camera detection. Device/OS support varies; check the current official page before giving model-specific steps. The reasoning limits below are conservative inferences, not Apple certification of this skill.

- [Apple: orange and green status indicators](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108331). Orange concerns an app using the iPhone microphone; green concerns its camera, possibly together with its microphone. These indicators do not report cameras or microphones elsewhere in a room.
- [Apple TN3111: iOS Wi-Fi API overview](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3111-ios-wifi-api-overview). Apple documents no general-purpose Wi-Fi scanning/configuration API on iOS and specialized limits/entitlements for APIs such as Hotspot Helper. This is not a claim that no authorized app can obtain any local-network information. Do not promise a general RF sweep, complete device inventory or arbitrary Wi-Fi scanning through an assistant.
- [Apple: Core Bluetooth discovery API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/corebluetooth/cbcentralmanager/scanforperipherals(withservices:options:)). Discovery concerns peripherals advertising services. It does not establish a comprehensive census of powered electronics or identify recording functions. This skill consumes existing supplied results; it does not invoke that API.
- [Apple: local network privacy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3179-understanding-local-network-privacy). App access to local-network operations is subject to privacy controls. A permission denial or restricted view is missing coverage, not evidence that the room has no devices.
- [Apple: unwanted-tracking alerts](https://support.apple.com/en-ie/119874). Alerts apply to supported trackers under specified conditions, including being seen moving with a person over time. An alert is distinct from detecting a camera or microphone; no alert cannot clear a room. The cited guidance also notes borrowed items as a possible explanation. Preserve evidence and prioritize personal safety without diagnosing stalking solely from a screenshot.

Do not market the phone camera, flashlight, LiDAR, magnetometer or microphone as an all-purpose hidden-device detector. Seeing a glow or reflection in a phone image does not identify its source. No glow or reflection cannot exclude a device. Infrared visibility differs by camera/model/filter and emitter; no verified device-specific capability is asserted here. Do not prescribe the remote-control/IR trick or a mirror fingernail test as a reliable screening method. This skill has no direct sensor access, even if another host offers unrelated camera or microphone features.

Web retrieval may be unavailable or Apple pages may be rate limited/JavaScript-only. Use the linked references for bounded background, identify unverified current details, and do not invent a source check or API capability. Capability research is not authorization to install applications or start scans.
